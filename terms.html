When you place an order, the reward you are using is set aside straight away so that it can’t be spent twice. If you don’t finish paying, the order closes by itself after about 24 hours and the reward comes back to you.

5. Ordering food

The food in the app is made and sold by our own kitchens — Basilico, Goat Pizza and Protein Pizza — in London. Each kitchen delivers within its own area, using its own drivers, or you can collect from it.

When you place an order you are offering to buy. A kitchen can decline it, for example because it is closed, too busy or something has sold out; if it does, you are refunded in full. The contract for the food is made when the kitchen accepts your order and it shows “Confirmed” in “Your orders”.

Prices include VAT. A delivery order has a flat delivery fee set by the kitchen, shown in your basket before you pay, and is £20.00 minimum, counted on the food before your reward comes off. There is no fee and no minimum for collection.

Your reward is used automatically — “Use my reward” is switched on in your basket — and pays for the food and the delivery fee up to the amount you have. Anything left is charged to your card. The total is shown before you pay.

Once the kitchen has accepted your order it can’t be cancelled or changed because you have changed your mind: the kitchen may already be making your food. Before it is accepted, tell us from the order under “Something’s wrong with this order” and we’ll try to stop it, but we can’t promise to reach the kitchen in time. Check your basket, address and phone number before you place an order.

If the kitchen cancels your order, everything you paid by card is refunded to that card, and any reward you used is returned to you.

For delivery, give an address the driver can reach and a phone number you’ll answer. If the driver can’t deliver because the address is wrong or no one is there, the kitchen may not be able to try again, and the order may still be charged.

The app doesn’t show allergen information. If you have a food allergy or intolerance, please don’t order until you have the allergen details for the dishes you want: email support@yourpizzaperks.com with the kitchen and the dishes, well ahead of when you want to eat.

If something is wrong with your order — missing, wrong, not fit to eat, very late or never arrived — tell us from the order under “Something’s wrong with this order”, as soon as you can. We’ll look into it with the kitchen and put it right, with a refund where one is due. Your legal rights to food that is as described and fit to eat are unaffected.
