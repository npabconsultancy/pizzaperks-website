Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy explains what Basilico Limited (“we” and “us”) does with your personal information when you use the Pizza Perks app: what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what you can ask of us. We are the data controller for it, which means we decide how it is used and are answerable for it.

In short

  • We collect only what the service needs: your account, your addresses, your gift cards and your orders.
  • Your card details go to Stripe, our payment provider. We never see your full card number.
  • We don’t sell your information, show you adverts or send you marketing.
  • Your location is used only when you ask, to find the nearest kitchen, and isn’t saved.
  • You can see, change and delete your details yourself, in Settings.

1. What we collect, and why

Your account: your email address and a password. Sign-in is handled by Google’s Firebase service, which holds your password; we never see it. We keep your email address to identify your account and to answer you.

Your profile: your name, phone number and home address, which you give us after signing up, and any delivery addresses you save. We use them to deliver your orders, to show you the kitchens within reach and to tell you if you are outside the area we serve.

Your gift cards: which retailer, the amount, when you bought it, and the card itself, which our gift card partner issues and keeps. We use these to show your cards in your Wallet, to work out your cashback, and to sort out a card that doesn’t arrive or doesn’t work.

Your payments: your card details go straight to Stripe, our payment provider. Stripe tells us whether a payment went through, and may pass on the card’s type, its last four digits, its expiry date and the billing postcode — never the full number or the security code. We keep a record of each payment and each refund.

Your orders and your reward: what you ordered, from which kitchen, for delivery or collection, what you paid, how much of your reward you used, and every change to your reward. We use these to run your orders, to pay refunds and to keep the accounts the law requires.

Your location: if you tap “USE MY LOCATION”, the app asks your phone for your position once, to find the kitchens nearest to you. It is used for that and isn’t saved to your account. You can say no, and type a postcode instead.

Your camera: on the payment screen you can choose to scan your card instead of typing its number, and the app then asks your phone for the camera, for that and nothing else. The card is read on your phone to fill in the form. The picture isn’t kept and never reaches us. You can say no, and type the number instead.

A check that the app is genuine: before our server hands over the details of a gift card, the app proves it is the real Pizza Perks app, through a check run by Apple or Google and Google’s Firebase service. That sends a token about the app and the phone, not about you.

Messages you send us: when you email us from the app — about an order, a gift card or a question — your message, and the details the app puts into it to help us, are kept in our support mailbox.

Technical records: our server keeps short-lived logs of requests — the address your phone connects from, the time, what was asked for and whether it worked — for security and for finding faults. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

2. Our reasons in law

UK data protection law asks us to say which reason allows each use. Most of what we do is needed to provide the service you signed up for: your account, your gift cards, your orders and your payments. Keeping records of payments and orders, and checking for fraud, are things the law requires of us. Keeping the app secure, keeping logs and answering your messages are in our legitimate interests, and in yours. Your location is used only with the permission you give your phone, which you can take back in your phone’s settings at any time.

3. Who we share it with

We share your information only with the companies that help us run the service, each for its part and no more. Google (Firebase) handles sign-in and the check that the app is genuine. Stripe takes your card payments. Tillo, our gift card partner, issues your gift cards. Microsoft hosts our database (Azure, in the UK). Titan hosts our support mailbox. Fly.io hosts our server, in London.

The kitchen making your order gets your name, your phone number, your email address, the order and, for delivery, your address, through the ordering system it uses, so that it can make your food and the driver can find you and call you.

The retailer whose gift card you buy doesn’t get your details from us. When you use the card, you deal with the retailer under its own privacy policy.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t give it to advertisers. We would disclose it if the law required us to, or if the business changed hands, in which case the new owner would take on the same duties to you.

4. Where it is kept

Our server and our database are in the United Kingdom, and so is the ordering system our kitchens use. Google, Stripe and Titan may process information outside the UK; where they do, it is under the safeguards UK law recognises for sending personal information abroad.

5. How long we keep it

Your account details, for as long as your account is open.

When you delete your account, your name, phone number, addresses and sign-in are removed. Records of your payments and orders are kept without your details for 6 years, because tax law requires it, and so that a refund can still be traced.

Gift card records, for as long as the card is valid, and for the period our gift card partner and the law require.

Messages you send us, for as long as the matter they are about is open, and for a reasonable time after in case it comes up again.

Technical logs, for a short time only.

6. Your rights

You can see and change your name, phone number and addresses in Settings, and delete your account there by tapping “DELETE MY ACCOUNT”.

You also have the right to ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to limit how we use it, to object to a use of it, and to have a copy passed to another service. Email support@yourpizzaperks.com from the address on your account, so that we can tell it’s you, and we’ll answer within one month.

If you are not happy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office, the UK’s data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.

7. Keeping it safe

Everything between the app and our server travels encrypted. Card payments are handled entirely by Stripe. The details of a gift card are shown only to the genuine app, signed in to your account. If you think someone else has used your account, tell us straight away.

8. Children

The app is for people aged 18 or over. We don’t knowingly collect information about anyone younger.

9. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy when the app changes or the law does. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and the version in the app is always the current one. If a change matters to you, we’ll tell you in the app.
