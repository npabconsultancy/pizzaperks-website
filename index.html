How Pizza Perks works

The app is free, with no subscription and no fees.

  1. 1

    Buy an online gift card

    Pick a shop you already use and choose an amount. Nothing comes in the post, and you get the full value to spend at that shop.

  2. 2

    Cashback builds your reward

    Each retailer shows the cashback it pays before you buy. It joins your reward as soon as your gift card arrives, normally within seconds.

  3. 3

    Your pizza fills up, slice by slice

    Every gift card fills it a little more. Your reward doesn’t expire, and you can spend it on any order, however much or little you have.

  4. 4

    Your reward pays for the pizza

    Choose a London kitchen and order for pickup or delivery. Your reward comes off the bill, delivery included, and anything left is paid by card.

Good to know

Does it cost anything to use?

No. The app is free, with no subscription and no fees. You pay the value of the gift card you buy, nothing on top, and you get the full value to spend at that retailer.

How much cashback will I earn?

Each retailer pays its own rate. It is shown before you buy, and once you choose an amount the app shows what you’ll earn back, before you pay.

Are the gift cards genuine?

Yes. Each gift card is made new, for you, when your payment goes through. It is for the full amount you paid, and it is spent like any other gift card from that retailer, under the retailer’s own terms.

Does my reward expire?

No. Your reward doesn’t expire, and there’s no deadline for spending it. It stays on your account for as long as the account is open.

Can I take my reward as cash?

No. Your reward is a discount on food from our kitchens. It isn’t money, so it can’t be withdrawn, exchanged for cash or moved to another account.

Can I use Pizza Perks if I don’t live in London?

Partly. You can buy gift cards and earn cashback wherever you live, but your reward can only be spent at our London kitchens for now: you collect from one, or have your order delivered if you’re inside its delivery area.
